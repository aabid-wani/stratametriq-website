## 💡 Quick Tips for Maximum Engagement
1. **Always use images/videos:** Never post just text. Take a 15-second screen recording of your VS Code Simulator working on the website and attach it to your tweets and LinkedIn posts.
2. **Tag relevant tech:** If you used Next.js, tag @nextjs. If it's a VS Code tool, tag @code. 
3. **Engage with comments:** The algorithm rewards posts where the author replies to every single comment in the first hour.
